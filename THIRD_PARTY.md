# Third-party notices

The Introduction v13 prompt, rules, preamble and template are distributed
with the author's permission under this project's MIT license. The packaged
checker adapts the same release's checker for PDFium and stable source
ordering on Windows. Its integrity manifest records the executable shipped
with this version.

Python dependencies are installed as separate distributions and retain their
own license files. In particular, [pypdfium2](https://github.com/pypdfium2-team/pypdfium2)
and its PDFium binaries include upstream and third-party notices in their
wheel. TeX Live and its fonts are installed separately. The frontend dependency
notices are included with the built web assets.
