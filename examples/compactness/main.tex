\begin{abstract}
This teaching manuscript presents the sequential compactness criterion on
the unit interval. Its mathematical content is classical. It supplies a
short, redistributable example for extraction, including a historical
comparison and the exact main statement.
\end{abstract}
\section{Background and question}
The Bolzano--Weierstrass theorem states that every bounded sequence of
real numbers admits a convergent subsequence. We ask when an analogous
conclusion holds for sequences of real-valued continuous functions on
the closed interval $[0,1]$, with convergence in the supremum norm.
