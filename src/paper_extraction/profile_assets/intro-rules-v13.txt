MATHEMATICAL INTRODUCTION — v13
Release: intro-v13.0.0-rc1-20261023
Companions: preamble-v13.tex, template-v13.tex, prompt-v13.txt.

DELIVERABLE AND PRIORITY
Produce an English mathematical Introduction, at most THREE body pages, followed
by a separate bibliography. Deliver introduction.tex/pdf and the unchanged local
preamble. This is a SELECTIVE mathematical introduction, not a transcription of
all source statements. Source fidelity applies to the assertion actually selected;
it does not oblige inclusion of every clause of its source theorem or proof.
The current user task controls scope. These rules control selection and writing;
the preamble enforces numbering and rendered-size limits. No per-paper relaxation,
font reduction, negative spacing or hidden migration of excess text is allowed.

HARD STANDARD-PROFILE BUDGETS
U is the normal 11pt body baseline fixed at document start (about 14.08 TeX pt).
Height includes the heading, citation, prose, formulas, lists and internal spacing.
It is measured by TeX after actual typesetting, NOT guessed from source line count.
                         count       prose words/block   height   display rows
Theorem A, B, C           1–3         75                  8U       2
Theorem 1, 2, 3, 4        1–4         100                 12U      3
Definition 1, 2, ...      0–6         65                  7U       3
introparagraph           as needed   45                  4U       0
A resultgroup contains exactly one current theorem, at most TWO dedicated
Definitions and any indispensable connecting paragraph: combined height <=22U.
At most TWO genuinely shared Definitions may precede the result groups, <=14U
combined. All connecting paragraphs together <=16U. These combined limits prevent
splitting one excessive block into many small blocks or dumping it into a preamble.
The theorem may have at most TWO display groups; a historical theorem at most ONE;
a definition at most TWO. A display's source-row count is one plus each nonterminal
TeX line-break command, including nested cases/matrices. A trailing break adds zero.
Prose-word counting excludes math and citations but includes words inside math
\text/\mbox; rendered height remains binding regardless of word-count shortcuts.
These numerical values are v13's editorial contract, not claims of mathematical
importance. If faithful rewriting cannot meet them, report the scope/size conflict;
do not silently lower mathematical precision, omit an admitted major contribution,
or label an oversized document accepted. Never add filler to reach a count.

A. BIND THE TASK AND THE EVIDENCE
A1. Read all four matching companions and identify the exact original paper,
version, title and authors. Keep sources unchanged. In a development test bind the
four files, checker and supplied source snapshot by SHA-256. A multi-file TeX input
requires an explicitly declared source root; a root-file hash is not full binding.
Use distinct case-id--snapshot-prefix directories; occupied names receive a new
run suffix via atomic creation. Ordinary writing needs no separate audit artifact.
A2. Read the abstract, introduction, principal statements, important applications,
and the definitions/body statements needed to check candidates. Search for material
contributions, not only source Theorem 1.1. Inspect PDF pages where extraction makes
signs, constants or quantifiers unclear. A generated old Intro is an observed output,
not evidence for missing original mathematics. Do not audit all proofs by default.
A3. Record source locations for retained claims and definitions. Do not silently
supply unread external results or remembered background. A standard notation may
be fixed without inventing a new claim; a new substantive definition or historical
assertion needs supplied support or explicit authorization for another source.
Source accuracy, importance admission, compact presentation and engineering status
are separate questions. Reporting a manuscript is not certifying its proof/status.

B. SELECT AND SIMPLIFY THE MATHEMATICAL CORE
B1. Admit only the first two importance tiers, not the best two results in each
paper. Tier 1: a substantial resolution of a central problem/characterization.
Tier 2: a substantial independent theorem or important consequence for a recognizable
object class. Routine substitutions, proof stages, implementation lemmas and minor
refinements do not earn a slot. Environment names, difficulty and length do not grade
importance. Apply the same admission threshold to prose, examples and formal blocks.
B2. For each candidate identify: (i) the mathematical objects and exact conclusion;
(ii) the independent question it resolves or comparison it improves; (iii) the source
facts supporting that significance. A clean statement alone, or “useful/reusable”,
is insufficient. For a technical candidate ask whether its objects are identifiable
without being “the output of step k” of this proof. Construction-only accuracy,
support and waiting-time details cannot be promoted by renaming a Proposition.
B3. Separate each source statement into: core claim; necessary assumptions and
normalizations; definitions; explanatory repetitions; independent applications;
proof/construction refinements. Retain the selected core WITH its necessary data.
Move only genuine reusable concepts into Definitions; delete repetitions. Assess
independent applications separately. Omit construction formulas and witness choices
when the retained claim is an existence/comparison result that does not require them.
Do not disguise a long technical hypothesis package as a newly invented “admissible”
definition just to shorten a theorem. Definitions serve meaning, not length evasion.
B4. Keep source support distinct from admitted/excluded/unresolved significance.
An unresolved side branch is omitted with an external note, not called low-value.
If the only central candidate is accurate but admission remains open, mark central
admission unresolved and deliver a scoped explanation, not an accepted Intro.
If essential source data are missing, mark content partial. “No qualifying result”
is reserved for completed assessment, not missing evidence. These explanatory
artifacts do not pass the standard-profile checker and must not masquerade as tests.
B5. One current theorem states ONE core contribution, with at most TWO tightly
linked conclusion clauses (or one genuine equivalence with at most three items).
A core convergence theorem keeps its actual quantifiers, topology, multiplicity,
fixed/modulo symmetry and asserted rate. It need not absorb a separable weak-solution
representative theorem or every source Remark. Select the supported smooth/weak or
other part explicitly in the source attribution. A short derivation does not make
a major new application unimportant. Do not discard important applications for size.
B6. When a block exceeds its budget, act in this order: remove duplicate explanation
and unneeded “does not assert” tails; use already defined concepts; extract a genuine
missing definition; select the actual core assertion and separately assess optional
refinements; split only independently useful admitted contributions. Recheck the
22U group budget and the total page budget. Never delete a necessary condition or
reduce convergence strength. If no faithful compact arrangement exists, deliver a
clearly marked scope/size exception, not a passing document or a weakened fake theorem.

C. FIX THE READING STRUCTURE
C1. Use this reading order: brief setting/question; the minimum definitions needed
for the comparison; HISTORICAL THEOREMS A/B/C; current-result groups with local
Definitions followed by Theorems 1/2/3/4. Every selected important historical conclusion
MUST be formal, not merely a name or a prose synopsis. Choose two or three directly
relevant source-supported comparisons when available; one is sufficient when only
one qualifies. Never invent or pad history. If none can be sourced, report the gap
and do not claim a standard-profile acceptance. History must precede current results.
C2. Historical statements include actual assumptions and conclusions with compact
author/source attribution. A conjecture/question is not a historical theorem. State
a reported unpublished result as a reported manuscript assertion, not established
consensus. State indirect attribution once in the reference note. A bridging sentence
explains the mathematical difference; it does not retell the historical theorem.
C3. Define a core nonstandard property once in a numbered Definition. One complete
standard convention may be inline in an introparagraph. Each Definition has one
concept or one genuinely paired comparison, not several unrelated concepts. Separate
unrelated invariants rather than making an encyclopedic “definitions” block. Give
domain, criterion, quantifiers and essential normalization. Stop at background
language; do not recursively define every elementary word in the definition.
C4. Among source-supported formulations choose one whose specialized objects can
be identified from declared data. Replacing an unknown term by several unexplained
ones is not completion. Identify the new objects minimally or choose another supported
formulation. A definition required only by an unselected refinement stays out. A
complex concept may be split only at real conceptual boundaries, with <=2 dedicated
Definitions per resultgroup; a proof-specific checklist is not a conceptual split.
C5. Put all nonformal body prose in introparagraph. Its function is setting,
question, genuine contrast or necessary convention, not a result/definition overflow
channel. No proof roadmap, long technical tail, secondary-result Remark or negative
claim catalogue. Do not repeat a formula in a preview, a theorem and its paraphrase.
A whole paragraph that only restates the adjacent theorem is deleted.

D. WRITE COMPACT, CLOSED STATEMENTS
D1. All retained current contributions use the theorem environment, displayed as
Theorem 1, Theorem 2, etc., without a section prefix. This is uniform editorial
numbering, not a claim that an original Corollary/Proposition was an independent
source theorem. Preserve its original role and number in the source citation, or
in a compact “application” caption. History uses historical; concepts use definition.
Do not print Corollary 1.2/Proposition 1.2 or hand-type a fake numbered heading.
D2. A theorem consists of minimal objects/assumptions, the decisive statement and
only necessary scope. Keep tensor/endomorphism types, metric dependence, parameter
ranges, some/every and subsequence/full-family distinctions. Definitions and shared
notation need not be copied into every theorem. Explanations of ordinary convergence,
redundant “no extra assumption” sentences, derivations and explicit proof witnesses
are not appended. A limiting object's identity is not left to a vague pronoun.
D3. Each separated theorem must be readable with its precisely cited Definitions.
Restate a short indispensable setting; do not repeat the entire preceding theorem.
When a long premise recurs, define a genuine shared mathematical setting once within
the applicable budget, then name it explicitly. “As above”, “the previous theorem”
and a remote bare number are not substitutes for objects or assumptions. The packet
of theorem plus declared definitions is reusable; universal word-for-word duplication
is neither required nor permitted to defeat the budget.
D4. Keep a genuine equivalence inside ONE theorem with explicit equivalence wording
and an enumerate list of at most three complete items. Do not split a necessary
if-and-only-if or equality/rigidity package. Separately conditional conclusions need
their own attached hypotheses. The source number belongs in a short citation; the
reader heading does not carry a proof-section inventory.
D5. Main results may be stated through a source-supported named criterion. An
important dimension-range/application theorem may select the existence consequence
without reproducing the product construction, special scaling representative or
proof of maximality. Mark the selected source part, keep its actual parameters and
conclusion, and do not claim complete coverage of the original multi-clause theorem.
Source fidelity means no alteration of what is retained, not exhaustive copying.
D6. Use displays for the core criterion or conclusion. For INDEPENDENT hypotheses,
use conditions with EVERY row beginning '&': their left edges agree. Put shared
index ranges in the preceding sentence; no detached far-right range column. For a
true calculation/equality chain use relations with exactly ONE relation anchor per
row. Do not use flalign, eqnarray, full-width arrays, \hfill or manual spacing to
stretch short formulas. Top-level align/gather/multline are unsupported: use the
provided two forms or one \[...\]. At most three source rows per display; no single
formula shrinkage, min/max towers with unexplained indices, or separated conditions.
D7. Use precise source locations without copying the source bibliography. Do not
add a proof-section qualification solely to lengthen a citation. Keep exact title
and all verified authors. No theorem's optional caption longer than 12 prose words;
citations are excluded from that word count but included in rendered height. All
facts in the reader text are source-supported; limitations belong in a separate note.

E. APPLY THE MEASURABLE PRESENTATION CONTRACT
E1. Use the unchanged local preamble-v13.tex and the executable Release assertion
from template-v13.tex. A4, 11pt, 22mm, newtx typography stays fixed. No font, margins,
spacing, counter, environment or budget redefinitions. The supported engine is
pdfLaTeX/T1 Latin text plus standard TeX math; unsupported Unicode must be disclosed,
not silently translated. Deliver installation requirements, never system font files.
E2. Every body block uses historical, definition, theorem or introparagraph. Each
current theorem is inside its own resultgroup. Only up to two genuinely SHARED
definitions are outside such groups. Dedicated definitions go immediately before
their theorem inside that group. The public preamble measures natural block height
and rejects excess; the checker also enforces counts, word/display budgets and
forbidden bypasses. Do not invoke raw internal environments or put body text outside
measured blocks. Do not raise limits to force a failed sample through.
E3. Bounded blocks are kept together with their heading and formulas; they are NOT
visibly boxed. Blocks are measured once and output unchanged. Never split one claim
into sequential theorem fragments for formatting. Definitions are not footnotes;
no unsupported material is concealed in references. Ordinary white space at the end
of a body page is acceptable; do not force one page or pad three pages.
E4. Put References on a new page with the profile's invisible markers and inline
bibliography. The checker verifies that its first page contains no preceding body
content. A mixed page counts toward the body cap and fails the separation check.
Do not override a failed detection with an assumed “pages minus one”.
E5. Inspect every final page: aligned hypotheses must share a visible left edge;
relation chains must share their relation column; index ranges stay with their
condition; no overfull/clipped formulas, tall isolated operators, or orphaned source
citations. A log measuring height cannot judge the visual composition by itself.
Record observed defects and repair the affected passage, without changing typography.

F. FINISH AND TEST HONESTLY
F1. Use one sequence: identify inputs; decide contributions; assign the finite block
budget; write the compact source-grounded core; compile/check; inspect pages; repair
concrete defects. No fixed rewrite quota, multi-agent review or score database is
required. Record a first complete draft only for development, not every ordinary run.
F2. Before acceptance check EACH retained theorem's core/assumption/definition/refinement
split, historical formalization, exact closed statement and dependent definitions.
Check excluded proof refinements did not re-enter as prose or artificial definitions.
Check that admitted major applications were not lost merely for space. Word/height
PASS is not proof of source accuracy or a tier grade; do those source-based decisions.
F3. Compile twice with -halt-on-error -file-line-error -no-shell-escape -recorder.
Use a third run only for actual cross-reference requests. A rebuild invalidates old
PDF/state BEFORE preflight. Verify the executed release, actually read profile,
input snapshots, resolved references, required measured-block telemetry, flat
numbering and all budgets. Any mandatory engineering check fails => nonzero exit,
no accepted deliverable PDF. compile-only is explicit and does not prove source binding.
F4. Deliver current supported source, its accepted PDF and local dependency, with
verified paths. Distinguish content selection, source support, deterministic format,
compilation and human/assistant visual inspection. Do not call a budget exception,
missing-history artifact or unresolved central selection fully compliant. Disclose
same-context/self-check testing; do not invent independent Codex or human approval.
Mathematical importance is an editorial judgment with source reasons, never inferred
from a clean build. Preserve useful engineering safeguards; they serve this reader
contract and must not replace it.
