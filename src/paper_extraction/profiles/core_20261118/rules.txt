# Mathematical paper extraction rules

## Deliverable and budgets

Write an English mathematical introduction to the supplied version of the paper. Explain the central problem, select substantial contributions and applications, and state the mathematical relationship between results. Attach verifiable source locations. A theorem may retain a self-contained part of a larger source statement when the citation identifies the part selected. First decide whether the supplied work contains a qualifying current theorem. If none qualifies, return only the source-bound facts for a brief Chinese Markdown note: the research question, what was assessed, the reason and its locations. Distinguish a completed negative assessment from unavailable or unreadable primary evidence. End this path here. The English manuscript requirements below apply when at least one current result qualifies.

Aim for at most three body pages and use up to five when necessary for the mathematics or its explanation. Start References on a fresh page. Use the same typography throughout. The downloadable files use the input document's stem followed by `-extraction.tex` and `-extraction.pdf`. The TeX includes the matching preamble and compiles independently.

| Block | Number | Prose-word target | Maximum height | Display groups | Total display rows per block |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Historical theorem | 0–3, labelled A–C | 100 | 11U | 1 | 3 |
| Current theorem | One per admitted core contribution, numbered from 1 | 140 | 16U | 2 | 4 |
| Definition | 0–6, consecutively numbered | 90 | 10U | 2 | 4 |
| Connecting paragraph | As needed | 100 | 8U | 0 | 0 |

U is the normal 11 pt body baseline fixed at the start of the document. Height is measured after typesetting and includes the heading, citation, prose, formulas, lists and internal spacing. A result group holds one current theorem, up to two dedicated definitions, and relevant connecting prose within 32U. Up to two genuinely shared definitions may precede the result groups, within 20U together. All connecting paragraphs together fit within 40U.

Word counts guide editing. Count words outside mathematics and citations, including words in mathematical `\text` or `\mbox`. Count one display row plus one for each nonterminal TeX row break, including nested cases and matrix rows. A trailing break adds zero. Each display fits within four source rows and the block's total display budget. Preserve the meaning of the selected claims when arranging them. Use extra space for mathematical explanation where the source supports it.

## A. Bind the task and evidence

**A1.** Identify the exact source version, title and authors. For multi-file TeX, bind the declared source root, its full file tree and the main entry. Cite the same version throughout. The workbench preserves the source snapshot, rules, typesetting assets and checker used for this batch. Bind the matching files by hash for testing and recovery, and allocate each build a distinct directory.

**A2.** Read the abstract, introduction, principal statements, important applications, and the definitions/body statements needed to check candidates. Search for material contributions, not only source Theorem 1.1. Inspect PDF pages where extraction makes signs, constants or quantifiers unclear. A generated old Intro is an observed output, not evidence for missing original mathematics. Do not audit all proofs by default.

**A3.** Record source locations for retained claims and definitions. Do not silently supply unread external results or remembered background. A standard notation may be fixed without inventing a new claim; a new substantive definition or historical assertion needs supplied support or explicit authorization for another source. Source accuracy, importance admission, compact presentation and engineering status are separate questions. Reporting a manuscript is not certifying its proof/status.

## B. Select and simplify the mathematical core

**B1.** Admit only the first two importance tiers, not the best two results in each paper. Tier 1: a substantial resolution of a central problem/characterization. Tier 2: a substantial independent theorem or important consequence for a recognizable object class. Routine substitutions, proof stages, implementation lemmas and minor refinements do not earn a slot. Environment names, difficulty and length do not grade importance. Apply the same admission threshold to prose, examples and formal blocks. Read the author’s stated central questions and principal advances before ranking local statements. Select the smallest set that represents those advances and their genuinely independent applications. Most papers have few such results. For each fourth or later core, identify the material advance that would disappear if it were omitted. A paper with several exceptional independent advances may retain them all.

**B2.** For each candidate identify: (i) the mathematical objects and exact conclusion; (ii) the independent question it resolves or comparison it improves; (iii) the source facts supporting that significance. A clean statement alone, or “useful/reusable”, is insufficient. For a technical candidate ask whether its objects are identifiable without being “the output of step k” of this proof. Construction-only accuracy, support and waiting-time details cannot be promoted by renaming a Proposition. Identify the closest comparison supported by the source, the difference that remains after accounting for it, and why that difference matters independently of the proof. Direct evidence may include the introduction, a sharp example or an independent application elsewhere in the paper. A reformulation of the claim, usefulness as a lemma in another proof, and solvability on a named class do not by themselves establish significance. Keep supporting results in the evidence register when they serve only that role. Do not invent a historical comparison or score. For a source-labelled lemma to stand as a current result, identify an actual passage in which the authors foreground this result, establish a specific comparison, or demonstrate a separate application of the conclusion. Quote the independent role in the paper’s words. Its own statement and use as an input to another proof cannot supply that evidence. If the paper supplies no such anchor, retain it only as supporting evidence. This test allows central lemmas and substantial applications, regardless of their printed label.

**B3.** Separate each source statement into: core claim; necessary assumptions and normalizations; definitions; explanatory repetitions; independent applications; proof/construction refinements. Retain the selected core WITH its necessary data. Move only genuine reusable concepts into Definitions; delete repetitions. Assess independent applications separately. Omit construction formulas and witness choices when the retained claim is an existence/comparison result that does not require them. Do not disguise a long technical hypothesis package as a newly invented “admissible” definition just to shorten a theorem. Definitions serve meaning, not length evasion.

**B4.** Assess source support and mathematical significance separately. Distinguish admitted, excluded and unresolved candidates. Omit an unresolved side branch from the exposition and retain its source and open question in the candidate record. When essential data for a central claim remain unavailable, state the precise part supported by the paper and record the material uncertainty. Reserve a finding of ‘no qualifying result’ for a completed assessment. A paper without a qualifying historical comparison proceeds through ordinary delivery.

**B5.** One current theorem states ONE core contribution, with at most TWO tightly linked conclusion clauses (or one genuine equivalence with at most three items). A core convergence theorem keeps its actual quantifiers, topology, multiplicity, fixed/modulo symmetry and asserted rate. It need not absorb a separable weak-solution representative theorem or every source Remark. Select the supported smooth/weak or other part explicitly in the source attribution. A short derivation does not make a major new application unimportant. Do not discard important applications for size.

**B6.** When a block exceeds its budget, remove repeated explanations and unnecessary qualifications first. Reuse concepts already defined, then extract a genuinely needed definition if one is missing. Select the actual core assertion and separately assess optional refinements. Split only independently useful admitted contributions. Recheck the 32U group budget and the body page limit. Preserve necessary conditions, convergence strength and independent applications. If a layout defect persists at delivery, keep the diagnostic with the actual delivered version.

## C. Organize the reader's introduction

**C1.** Open with the setting and the question. Introduce the minimum shared definitions needed for comparison. Retain historical comparisons that are directly relevant, verifiable in the supplied paper and help the reader understand a current contribution. If more than three qualify, select the three that best explain the present advance. State the selected results formally and label them A, B, C in reading order. One qualifying comparison is sufficient. If none qualifies, proceed directly to the current contributions without a history heading, comment or warning. Put local definitions immediately before the corresponding current theorem. Number the current theorems consecutively from 1.

**C2.** Give each selected historical theorem its assumptions, conclusion and compact author/source attribution. Preserve the status of an unpublished manuscript or a result reported second-hand. Identify indirect attribution once where relevant to the citation. Treat conjectures and questions as such. Use a bridging sentence to explain the mathematical difference from the present result. A cited definition, technique or construction with no separate historical theorem belongs in background prose. Put original authorship and any secondary reporting locator together in the historical heading. Anchor an independently printed historical comparison to the actual passage that establishes both its role and the contrast with this paper. A bare attribution of a definition or construction is background.

**C3.** Define a core nonstandard property once in a numbered Definition. One complete standard convention may be inline in an introparagraph. Each Definition has one concept or one genuinely paired comparison, not several unrelated concepts. Separate unrelated invariants rather than making an encyclopedic “definitions” block. Give domain, criterion, quantifiers and essential normalization. Stop at background language; do not recursively define every elementary word in the definition.

**C4.** Among source-supported formulations choose one whose specialized objects can be identified from declared data. Replacing an unknown term by several unexplained ones is not completion. Identify the new objects minimally or choose another supported formulation. A definition required only by an unselected refinement stays out. A complex concept may be split only at real conceptual boundaries, with <=2 dedicated Definitions per resultgroup; a proof-specific checklist is not a conceptual split.

**C5.** Put nonformal body prose in introparagraph blocks. Use these paragraphs for the setting, central question, motivation, a necessary convention, a genuine comparison, or an explanation of the scope and connections of results. Ground the exposition in the paper. Remove paragraphs that merely restate the adjacent theorem or repeat its formula. Keep proof roadmaps, construction details and secondary assertions that fail admission outside the reader text. Place an explanation comparing the present theorem with earlier work after the theorem it explains. Bind the comparative source to that group separately from the statement’s own source.

## D. Write statements that stand on their own

**D1.** All retained current contributions use the theorem environment, displayed as Theorem 1, Theorem 2, etc., without a section prefix. This is uniform editorial numbering, not a claim that an original Corollary/Proposition was an independent source theorem. Preserve its original role and number in the source citation, or in a compact “application” caption. History uses historical; concepts use definition. Do not print Corollary 1.2/Proposition 1.2 or hand-type a fake numbered heading.

**D2.** A theorem consists of minimal objects/assumptions, the decisive statement and only necessary scope. Keep tensor/endomorphism types, metric dependence, parameter ranges, some/every and subsequence/full-family distinctions. Definitions and shared notation need not be copied into every theorem. Explanations of ordinary convergence, redundant “no extra assumption” sentences, derivations and explicit proof witnesses are not appended. A limiting object's identity is not left to a vague pronoun.

**D3.** Each separated theorem must be readable with its precisely cited Definitions. Restate a short indispensable setting; do not repeat the entire preceding theorem. When a long premise recurs, define a genuine shared mathematical setting once within the applicable budget, then name it explicitly. “As above”, “the previous theorem” and a remote bare number are not substitutes for objects or assumptions. The packet of theorem plus declared definitions is reusable; universal word-for-word duplication is neither required nor permitted to defeat the budget.

**D4.** Keep a genuine equivalence inside ONE theorem with explicit equivalence wording and an enumerate list of at most three complete items. Do not split a necessary if-and-only-if or equality/rigidity package. Separately conditional conclusions need their own attached hypotheses. The source number belongs in a short citation; the reader heading does not carry a proof-section inventory.

**D5.** Main results may be stated through a source-supported named criterion. An important dimension-range/application theorem may select the existence consequence without reproducing the product construction, special scaling representative or proof of maximality. Mark the selected source part, keep its actual parameters and conclusion, and do not claim complete coverage of the original multi-clause theorem. Source fidelity means no alteration of what is retained, not exhaustive copying.

**D6.** Use displays for the core criterion or conclusion. For INDEPENDENT hypotheses, use conditions with EVERY row beginning '&': their left edges agree. Put shared index ranges in the preceding sentence; no detached far-right range column. For a true calculation/equality chain use relations with exactly ONE relation anchor per row. Do not use flalign, eqnarray, full-width arrays, \hfill or manual spacing to stretch short formulas. Top-level align/gather/multline are unsupported: use the provided two forms or one \[...\]. At most four source rows per display, within the block's total row budget; no single formula shrinkage, min/max towers with unexplained indices, or separated conditions.

**D7.** Cite the exact original statement, section or page wherever the source permits. For TeX inputs choose a verified statement or section locator when available. Keep filenames and source-line ranges in the workbench evidence record. If only the bibliographic identity can be verified, cite that identity. Give the exact title and all verified authors. Include only references used by the introduction. Keep every optional theorem caption within 12 prose words. Exclude citations from that count and include them in the rendered height. Keep the limitations of the extraction in its accompanying record. Each formal theorem, historical theorem and definition carries its source in the heading note, including the bibliography citation and verified locator. Distinguish original attribution, the paper reporting it, and a comparison. The block contains the mathematical statement; the same source note does not reappear at its end. Preserve ordinary mathematical brackets.

## E. Apply the typesetting contract

**E1.** Use the matching preamble and executable release assertion of this batch's template. Fix A4, 11 pt, 22 mm margins and newtx typography. Use pdfLaTeX/T1 text and standard TeX mathematics. Preserve fonts, spacing, counters, environments and budgets. Encode supported Unicode symbols in TeX without changing their meaning and record any unresolved character that matters mathematically. Document reproduction requirements. Embed the matching preamble in the exported TeX.

**E2.** Use historical, definition, theorem or introparagraph for each body block. Put each current theorem in its own resultgroup. Place at most two genuinely shared definitions outside the groups and dedicated definitions immediately before their theorem. Use the public measured environments. The checker enforces counts, natural block height, display budgets and supported layout. Word targets guide editing. Keep all body material inside its measured blocks, with the same size limits throughout the batch.

**E3.** Bounded blocks are kept together with their heading and formulas; they are NOT visibly boxed. Blocks are measured once and output unchanged. Never split one claim into sequential theorem fragments for formatting. Definitions are not footnotes; no unsupported material is concealed in references. Ordinary white space at the end of a body page is acceptable; do not force one page or pad three pages.

**E4.** Put References on a new page with the profile's invisible markers and inline bibliography. The checker verifies that its first page contains no preceding body content. A mixed page counts toward the body cap and fails the separation check. Do not override a failed detection with an assumed “pages minus one”.

**E5.** On the ordinary review path inspect every rendered page. Check that independent hypotheses share a left edge, relations share their column and index ranges remain attached to their conditions. Look for clipped or overfull formulas, isolated tall operators and orphaned citations. Record the actual page, affected passage and observed defect so the writer can correct it locally. Reuse a visual observation only for matching page content and settings. On the third-draft delivery path report the checks actually performed on the delivered version. A wrap between ordinary words, a term and its inline symbol, or an isolated last word of ordinary prose is acceptable when everything remains readable and correctly attached. Request another draft only for a visible defect that affects mathematical reading, such as an obscured symbol, clipped line, overlapping material, detached condition or orphaned source note. Describe the concrete effect.

## F. Review, compile and deliver

**F1.** Identify inputs, decide contributions, allocate the block budgets and write the first complete source-grounded draft. Review content, compile, check layout and inspect pages on the ordinary path. Deliver immediately when the checks pass. Use the second draft to repair concrete defects and recheck the affected material. Use the third draft to resolve outstanding findings with the available evidence and proceed directly to local delivery. Each automatic run has at most three writing rounds. A local recompilation requires no model call.

**F2.** During content review check each retained theorem's core, assumptions, definitions and optional refinements. Check formal statements and attribution for chosen historical comparisons and ensure that each current theorem can be read with its declared definitions. Check that excluded proof refinements have not returned as prose or artificial concepts and that admitted major applications were not lost for space. Judge source accuracy and mathematical importance from the original evidence. Compare local repairs with their bound passages and trace changes in definitions or numbering to dependent statements. Check against the approved core register. A substantive error in a selected statement warrants a targeted repair. Nominate any genuinely missing central contribution for a source-bound selection decision before it may enter the draft. Editorial preference alone does not open another round. Request a further source reading only for a concrete gap in the supplied evidence.

**F3.** Compile the selected TeX twice with pdfLaTeX using the same inputs. The checked build uses -halt-on-error, -file-line-error, -no-shell-escape and -recorder. Use another pass only for an actual cross-reference request. Start each build with fresh outputs. Preserve earlier artifacts under their own draft identities. Verify the executed profile, source binding, resolved references, measured blocks, numbering and the actual PDF pages. Attach diagnostics to this build. If the final TeX fails to compile, repair and recompile locally or use a previously verified version with its matching TeX and PDF.

**F4.** Deliver an editable standalone TeX file and its corresponding readable PDF, each named with the input stem and -extraction. Embed the matching preamble in the exported TeX. Use the chosen batch name for its ZIP. Associate hashes, draft round and observed checks with the actual delivered files, including any earlier version or simplified layout used. Distinguish content review, source support, formatting, compilation and visual review in the record. Attribute human or independent review only when performed. Judge mathematical importance from the source reasons.
